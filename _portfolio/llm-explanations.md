---
title: "Explaining LLM behaviour and limitations"
excerpt: "How do large language models use their context, what biases do they carry, and how well do they reason?"
collection: portfolio
---

We study the behavior and limitations of LLMs, with a focus on [model bias](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0277640) (including [political bias](https://aclanthology.org/2025.ijcnlp-short.25/)), [reasoning abilities](https://aclanthology.org/2022.coling-1.8/), and whether [highlight explanations reveal context utilisation](https://direct.mit.edu/coli/article/doi/10.1162/COLI.a.621/136361/Evaluation-Framework-for-Highlight-Explanations-of).
