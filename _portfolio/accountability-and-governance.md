title: "Accountability, law and governance"
excerpt: "How can we audit what language models have learned and used, and check AI systems against data-protection and AI regulation?"
collection: portfolio
order: 4
---

Laws such as the GDPR and the EU AI Act place obligations on how AI systems are built and deployed, but checking a model against those obligations is an open technical problem. We bring interpretability and evaluation methods to questions of accountability: auditing what a model has learned and relies on, and turning legal requirements into evidence that can be measured.

This work is a collaboration with Supreeth Shastri's Lawful Computing Lab at UNT, which works at the intersection of computer systems and law.
