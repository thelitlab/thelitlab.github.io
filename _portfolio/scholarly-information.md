title: "Scholarly information processing"
excerpt: "Better tools for digital libraries: figures, reproducibility, limitations and impact of scientific papers."
collection: portfolio
---

We build methods that enhance users' experience with digital libraries, for example [extracting information from scientific figures](https://dl.acm.org/doi/pdf/10.1145/2928294.2928305), [understanding reproducibility of scientific articles](https://dl.acm.org/doi/pdf/10.1145/3627673.3679831), [automatically generating limitations of scientific papers](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.18207), and [predicting scholarly impact](https://aclanthology.org/2025.sdp-1.11/).
