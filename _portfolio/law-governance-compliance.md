Laws such as the GDPR and the EU AI Act place obligations on how AI systems are built and how they use data, but showing that a model meets those obligations is an open technical problem. We study how interpretability can supply evidence for compliance claims: auditing what data a model has learned from and relies on, and turning legal requirements into properties that can be checked.

This work is a collaboration with Supreeth Shastri's Lawful Computing Lab at UNT, which works at the intersection of computer systems and law.

**Keywords:** data use audit · verifiable compliance
