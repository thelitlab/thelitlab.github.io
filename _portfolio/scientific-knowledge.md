We build tools and resources for scientific articles, figures, tables, and claims. Our work addresses information extraction, scientific hallucination detection, reproducibility, research limitations, and scholarly impact.

Examples include [extracting information from scientific figures](https://dl.acm.org/doi/pdf/10.1145/2928294.2928305), [understanding reproducibility in scientific articles](https://dl.acm.org/doi/pdf/10.1145/3627673.3679831), [generating limitations of scientific papers](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.18207), and [predicting scholarly impact](https://aclanthology.org/2025.sdp-1.11/).
