title: "Understanding model behavior"
excerpt: "How do language models represent, use, and explain information when they produce an answer or judgment?"
collection: portfolio
order: 1
---

We study what language models encode and use when they answer questions, assess responses, or generate explanations. Our work examines representations, reasoning, context utilization, feature interactions, and the relationship between an explanation and the computation that produced a decision.

Representative topics include [reasoning abilities](https://aclanthology.org/2022.coling-1.8/), [model bias](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0277640), and whether [highlight explanations reveal context utilization](https://direct.mit.edu/coli/article/doi/10.1162/COLI.a.621/136361/Evaluation-Framework-for-Highlight-Explanations-of).
