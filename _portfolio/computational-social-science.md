Researchers increasingly give language models the questionnaires and survey instruments designed for people, and read the answers as evidence of a model's opinions or biases. We ask whether such instruments are valid and reliable for models: do the responses reflect a stable internal stance, or the wording and format of the question?

Our work includes [a factor analysis of the Political Compass Test for LLMs](https://aclanthology.org/2025.ijcnlp-short.25/), which examines what this widely used instrument actually measures, and [quantifying gender bias towards politicians in cross-lingual language models](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0277640).

**Keywords:** instrument validity · model internal stance · social and political bias
