We develop datasets and methods for evaluating language-model outputs with human preferences, rubrics, and model-based judges. We study [long-form question answering](https://aclanthology.org/2026.lrec-1.425/), [student response assessment](https://aclanthology.org/2026.lrec-1.368/), [evaluative reasoning, and the extent to which generated rationales support or merely accompany a judgment](WIP).
