This is where lab members write about their research, papers, tools, and things they learned along the way.

Anyone in the lab can publish here: add one Markdown file to `_posts/` and open a pull request. The [contributing guide](https://github.com/{{ site.repository }}/blob/{{ site.repository_branch }}/CONTRIBUTING.md) has a template and walks through the steps.

Posts support everything the rest of the site does: code blocks,

```python
print("hello from the lab")
```

math via MathJax, like $$e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0$$, tables, and images.
